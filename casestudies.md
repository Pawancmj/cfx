## 1. Income Tax Department (Investigation Division)

### Client
Government Agency – Income Tax Department (Investigation Wing)

### Industry
Government / Law Enforcement / Financial Investigation

### Objective
To support ongoing investigations by conducting forensically sound data acquisition, analysis, and evidence extraction from digital devices during search and seizure operations.

### Challenges
- Handling live search & seizure environments  
- Ensuring legal compliance and evidence integrity  
- Working with large volumes of heterogeneous data  
- Time-sensitive imaging at on-premise locations  
- Maintaining strict chain of custody  

### Approach & Methodology

#### 1. On-Site Imaging (During Search Operations)
- Conducted forensic imaging of devices (laptops, desktops, storage media) at premises  
- Used write blockers and industry-standard tools  
- Ensured hash verification (MD5/SHA) for data integrity  

#### 2. Secure Evidence Handling
- Maintained chain of custody documentation  
- Proper labeling, sealing, and logging of devices  

#### 3. Data Extraction & Processing
- Performed logical and physical data extraction  
- Parsed structured and unstructured datasets  

#### 4. Forensic Analysis
- Timeline analysis  
- Keyword searching & filtering  
- Identification of financial discrepancies and hidden data  
- Recovery of deleted artifacts  

### Tools & Technologies Used
- Magnet AXIOM  
- Cellebrite Physical Analyzer  
- Oxygen Forensics  
- X-Ways Forensics  

### Outcome
- Successfully supported multiple high-value investigations  
- Delivered court-admissible forensic reports  
- Enabled identification of critical financial evidence  
- Improved investigation efficiency and turnaround time  

### Impact
✔ Strengthened evidence collection process  
✔ Ensured legal compliance  
✔ Accelerated investigation workflows

## 2. TDS Department – Training & Capability Building

### Client
TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Department  

### Industry
Government / Financial Compliance  

### Objective
To enhance the technical capability of staff in handling digital systems, ERP platforms, and forensic tools.  

### Challenges
- Lack of hands-on experience with modern forensic tools  
- Limited exposure to ERP systems like SAP  
- Need for practical, real-world training  

### Approach

#### 1. Training Needs Assessment
- Evaluated skill gaps across teams  
- Identified key tools and platforms used in daily operations  

#### 2. Customized Training Program
Delivered structured training on:
- Digital Forensics Fundamentals  
- Data handling & evidence awareness  
- ERP Systems (SAP and others)  
- Software usage for investigation and compliance  

#### 3. Hands-On Workshops
- Real-world scenarios and simulations  
- Practical exercises on forensic tools  
- Case-based learning approach  

### Tools Covered
- SAP ERP Systems  
- Digital forensic tools (AXIOM, Cellebrite, etc.)  
- Data analysis platforms  

### Outcome
- Improved technical proficiency of staff  
- Increased efficiency in handling digital evidence and ERP data  
- Reduced dependency on external experts  

### Impact
✔ Enhanced internal investigation capabilities  
✔ Better data handling & compliance awareness  
✔ Empowered workforce with practical skills

## 3. AKKD Services – Corporate Cybersecurity & Forensics Awareness

### Client
AKKD Services (Corporate Organization)  

### Industry
Corporate / Business Services  

### Objective
To educate employees on digital forensics importance, identify system vulnerabilities, and improve data protection practices.  

### Challenges
- Lack of awareness about digital risks and threats  
- Poor data handling practices  
- Vulnerabilities in existing workflows  

### Approach

#### 1. Security Assessment
- Identified gaps in:
  - Data handling  
  - Access control  
  - System usage practices  

#### 2. Awareness & Training Sessions
- Conducted sessions on:
  - Importance of digital forensics  
  - Common cyber threats  
  - Insider risks  
  - Data leakage scenarios  

#### 3. Risk Identification & Prevention
- Highlighted operational and technical loopholes  
- Recommended best practices:
  - Secure data storage  
  - Access control policies  
  - Monitoring mechanisms  

### Outcome
- Increased employee awareness of cybersecurity risks  
- Reduced chances of data breaches and mishandling  
- Improved organizational readiness for incidents  

### Impact
✔ Stronger data protection culture  
✔ Reduced internal vulnerabilities  
✔ Better preparedness for cyber incidents
